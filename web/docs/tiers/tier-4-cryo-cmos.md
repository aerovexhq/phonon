# Tier 4: Cryogenic Cryo-CMOS Physics

Tier 4 extends semiconductor modeling down to deep cryogenic temperatures (4.2 K liquid Helium down to sub-100 mK dilution refrigerator stages).

## Physics & Phenomena
- **Incomplete Dopant Ionization & Freeze-Out**: Non-linear carrier concentration reductions with donor and acceptor freeze-out energy levels.
- **Subthreshold Swing Steepening**: Sub-60 mV/decade subthreshold swing at cryogenic temperatures.
- **Threshold Voltage Shifts**: Bandgap widening and Fermi level movement toward band edges.
- **Quantum Interface States**: Band tail hopping conduction and ballistic transport components.
