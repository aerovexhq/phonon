# Tier 0: Topological Quantum Acoustics

Tier 0 represents Phonon's highest abstraction level for topological quantum computing, quantum acoustics, and phononic metamaterials.

## Key Physical Capabilities
- **Non-Abelian Anyon Braiding**: Simulates exchange statistics and braiding operators for Fibonacci anyons, Ising anyons, and parafermions.
- **Acoustically Levitated Artificial Atoms**: High-frequency Surface Acoustic Wave (SAW) and Bulk Acoustic Wave (BAW) standing-wave traps for superconducting qubits and nanodiamond NV centers.
- **Topological Phononic Metamaterials**: Bandgap phononic crystals providing mechanical and thermal isolation against decoherence.
- **Tripartite Entanglement Swapping**: Acoustically mediated magnon-phonon and photon-phonon quantum repeater nodes.
- **Master Equation Density Matrix Integration**: High-throughput Lindblad master equation solvers tracking coherence retention, dephasing rates, and gate fidelities.
