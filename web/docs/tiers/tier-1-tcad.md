# Tier 1: Microscopic TCAD & Poisson-Drift-Diffusion

Tier 1 provides microscopic device-level Technology Computer-Aided Design (TCAD) finite-difference mesh physics.

## Physical Models
- **1D/2D Non-Uniform Finite Difference Mesh**: Dynamic adaptive node distribution around junctions and dielectric interfaces.
- **Non-Linear Poisson Equation**: Fully resolved electrostatic potential profiles with Fermi-Dirac electron and hole distribution functions.
- **Drift-Diffusion Current Continuity**: Carrier drift under local electric fields coupled with spatial gradient diffusion.
- **Band Bending & Short-Channel Effects**: Drain-Induced Barrier Lowering (DIBL), velocity saturation, and surface mobility degradation.
